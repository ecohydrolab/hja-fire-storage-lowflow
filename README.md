# hja-fire-storage-lowflow
HJA storage-fire impacts on low flows workflow and paper notes

## Main idea
Used streamflow data across watersheds with varying subsurface storage at the HJ Andrews to evaluate how low flow and recession curves change following the Holiday Farm and Lookout Creek fires. 

The paper shows that:
  - Vegetation mortality drives higher post-fire low flows by reducing evapotranspiration
  - High subsurface storage mediates wildfire impacts on summer streamflow
  - Initial wildfire increases summer streamflow are persistent

## Questions
  - How does wildfire affect annual low flows?
  - How does wildfire affect summer baseflow recession rates?
  - How does subsurface storage and burn severity mediate watershed responses?
